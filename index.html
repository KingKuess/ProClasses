ProClasses Guide

Your primary weapon picks your class. Armor, perks and gear are assigned when you spawn.

This server runs a class mod

Standard loadouts do not work here. Whatever weapon is in your first slot when you spawn decides your class — every class in the roster below has fixed armor tiers and perks. Spawn with anything else and you get the Default class: full plate and a greatsword.

Step 1

Pick a roster weapon

Put one of the weapons below in your first weapon slot. Or open your armory after joining — ready-made loadouts appear under the "Server Classes" category, one per weapon.

Step 2

Spawn — the class is applied

Armor is set to the class tiers, perks are replaced, extra gear is removed (Rats get a firepot). Armor you already have at the right tier is kept, so your cosmetics survive. Your saved loadouts are never edited.

Warning

No roster weapon = Default

Fists, no weapon, or a weapon that isn't in the roster puts you in the Default class: 3/3/3 armor and your weapon is swapped for a greatsword.

How the class is applied

  • Once per life. The class is decided by the weapon in your first slot when you spawn (or when the match re-outfits you at round start).
  • Hand-swapping does nothing. Drawing a secondary, or picking a weapon up off the ground mid-round, never changes your class or your armor.
  • Re-picking during the pre-round grace period works. In Skirmish you can switch loadouts before the round starts and the new class is applied.
  • Armor: each slot is set to the class tier. Right-tier pieces are kept (cosmetics preserved); wrong-tier pieces are replaced with the tier defaults above.
  • Perks: fully replaced by the class perks. Gear: carried secondaries are removed; Rat gets a firepot.
  • Fists or no weapon: you are still put in the Default class and handed a greatsword — fists are never taken away.

Why a class mod?

  • Weapon choice and armor are tied together, so every weapon has a defined role instead of everyone running the same 3/3/3 build.
  • Strong, expensive weapons (Zweihander, Halberd, Spear, Polehammer) come with light armor; one-handers get mobility perks and a firepot; everything else is full plate.
  • You still customize: the mod only replaces armor at the wrong tier, so the look of your kit is yours.
