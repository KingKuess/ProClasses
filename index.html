02How it's applied

  1. Once per life — decided by your first-slot weapon at spawn (and at round-start re-outfits).
  2. Hand-swaps do nothing — drawing a secondary or picking a weapon up mid-round never re-classes you.
  3. Grace-period re-picks count — switch loadouts before a Skirmish round starts and the new class applies.
  4. Perks are replaced by the class perks; carried secondaries are removed (Rat gets a firepot).
  5. Armor is set per slot to the class tier — your own right-tier pieces stay, so your look survives.
