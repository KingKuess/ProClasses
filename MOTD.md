# THE KUNGLE - ProClasses

**Your first-slot weapon picks your class.** Armor, perks and gear are set when you spawn. Right-tier armor you already wear is kept. Off-roster weapon, fists or no weapon = **3/3/3 + greatsword**.

## Support - 1/3/1 - Brawler, Stun, Acrobat

Zweihander, Halberd, Spear, Polehammer, Partisan, Billhook

## Rat - 2/1/1 - Brawler, Stun, Second Wind, Dodge, Acrobat - Firepot

Eveningstar, Bastard Sword, Mace, Heavy Handaxe, Arming Sword, Warhammer *(banned here: Rapier, Short Spear, Falchion)*

## Vanguard - 3/3/3 - Brawler, Stun

Bardiche, Greatsword, Maul, Executioner's Sword, Estoc, Falx, Poleaxe, War Axe, Battle Axe, Messer, Longsword *(banned here: Quarterstaff)*

## Armor you get (wrong-tier slots only)

- **T1** Peasant Hat - Coat of Plates (leather) - Rider's Hosen
- **T2** Eisenhut - Chainmail Shirt - Calf Guard Hosen
- **T3** Flat Templar - Crude Cuirass - Crude Chausses

## Rules

1. Once per life - decided by your first-slot weapon at spawn.
2. Hand-swaps and mid-round pickups never re-class you. Loadout re-picks before a Skirmish round starts do.
3. Perks are replaced by the class perks. Carried secondaries are removed.
4. Ready-made loadouts are in your armory under **Server Classes** (e.g. *1/3/1 Zweihander Support*).
