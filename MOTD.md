*If the image did not load:* your first-slot weapon picks your class. Armor, perks and gear are set when you spawn; right-tier armor you already wear is kept. Off-roster weapon, fists or no weapon = **3/3/3 + greatsword**.

- **Support 1/3/1** (Brawler, Stun, Acrobat): Billhook, Halberd, Partisan, Polehammer, Spear, Zweihander
- **Rat 2/1/1** (Brawler, Stun, Second Wind, Dodge, Acrobat + Firepot): Arming Sword, Bastard Sword, Battle Axe, Eveningstar, Heavy Handaxe, Mace, Warhammer *(banned here: Falchion, Rapier, Short Spear)*
- **Vanguard 3/3/3** (Brawler, Stun): Bardiche, Estoc, Executioner's Sword, Falx, Greatsword, Longsword, Maul, Messer, Poleaxe, War Axe *(banned here: Quarterstaff)*
